
## Bandwidth: 
Is known as the capacity of a medium to carry data in a timeframe, to be specific, the amount of bit or data that can flow from one place to another in one second. Common bandwidth measurements are as follows:

* Thousands of bits per second: (Kbps)
* Millions of bits per second: (Mbps)
* Billions of bits per second: (Gbps)

## Throughput
Like bandwith troughput is the capacity of a medium to carry data in a timeframe. However, due to a number of factors, many factors inlfuence throughput including:

* The amount of data being sent and received over the connection 
* The types of data being transmitted 
* The **latency** created by the number of network devices encountered between source and destination.
